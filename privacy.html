Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

BossCat Uploader ("the app") is a private tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channels BossCat-HQ and Mona Off The Frame, for managing those channels only.

What data the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app requests access to the owner's own YouTube account through the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API. It uses this access only to upload and schedule the owner's videos, edit their titles, descriptions and settings, reply to comments on the owner's channels, and read the owner's channel statistics.

What data the app stores

The app stores the OAuth access and refresh tokens for the owner's own channels on the owner's private server, readable only by the owner. It does not collect, store or process data about any other user. Public comments are read only so the owner can reply to them on their own channel; they are not stored or shared.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer any Google user data to third parties, and does not use it for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app also follows the YouTube Terms of Service; see the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access deletes the app's ability to use the stored tokens.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact details on the About page of either YouTube channel.
